## Where People Lose Cashback — Common Tracking Traps and Fixes

The cashback system is reliable when it works, but it's fragile. Here's where it breaks, and what to do about it.

**Trap 1 — You opened Vrbo directly first.** If you visited Vrbo in another tab before clicking through ShopBack, the last-click attribution may go to that direct visit, and ShopBack's tracking won't fire. Fix: always start your booking session from the ShopBack Vrbo merchant page. If you accidentally opened Vrbo first, clear your cookies or restart your browser, then click through fresh.

**Trap 2 — You used an adblocker, VPN, or another cashback extension.** Any of these can intercept the tracking cookie. Fix: disable adblockers and VPNs for the booking session, and don't run competing cashback extensions simultaneously. Pick one portal per booking.

**Trap 3 — You clicked a Facebook ad or Google ad on the way to checkout.** Those clicks can also claim attribution and override the ShopBack click. Fix: don't click any third-party links between the ShopBack click and the Vrbo checkout.

**Trap 4 — You didn't accept all cookies on Vrbo.** Vrbo can only confirm a transaction to ShopBack if you accept all cookies on their pages. Fix: accept cookies when prompted. Don't reject them in the cookie banner.

**Trap 5 — Your cart wasn't empty.** Vrbo cashback requires a clean session. If you had a previous property sitting in your cart, the booking may not track cleanly. Fix: clear your cart, close the Vrbo app, then click through from ShopBack and complete the purchase fresh.

**Trap 6 — You didn't file a missing cashback claim.** Sometimes tracking just doesn't fire, for no obvious reason. Fix: most aggregators, ShopBack included, allow you to submit a missing cashback claim. You'll need the click timestamp from your ShopBack account and the Vrbo order number from your confirmation email. File it as soon as you notice the cashback hasn't shown up as pending within the expected two-day window.
